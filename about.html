About SupaCheck

SupaCheck reviews final year project chapters for structure, citation, and formatting issues — checking each chapter against the standards Nigerian universities actually grade on. It does not generate or rewrite content; every fix stays in the student's own words.

Developed by

TCT Technologies

TCT Technologies is a Nigerian tech startup building AI-powered tools that make useful technology cheap and accessible for students and young professionals — starting with the everyday academic and career work that usually costs far more than it should.

Founder
